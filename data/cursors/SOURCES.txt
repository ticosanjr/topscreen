TopScreen cursor atlas - origins and licences
=============================================

Built-in theme cursors
  Extract from the installed XCursor themes (Bibata, Breeze, Adwaita, Yaru,
  DMZ, Google Dot, XCursor Pro) plus the procedural macOS/Windows/effect
  cursors. Regenerated by tools/make-cursor-atlas.py.

Custom cursor pack (69 PNG files)
  Source: Resources/cursors (PNG sources and manifest.json).
  The project owner supplied this pack and confirmed authorization to modify,
  pack, and redistribute it with TopScreen. Original artwork remains the
  property of its respective authors.
  File names and visible labels were normalized; the source manifest keeps the
  style order used by CursorStyle.
